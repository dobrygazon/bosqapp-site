# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-09-09*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Boşqabındakı yeməyin şəklini çək, kaloriləri və makronutriyentləri anında öyrən. (Snap a photo of your plate, instantly track calories and macros.)  
**What it does:** bosq is an AI-powered nutrition and calorie tracking mobile application. Users simply snap a photo of their meal, and bosq's computer vision and AI instantly identifies the dish, estimates portion sizes, and calculates calories along with macronutrients (proteins, fats, carbs, fiber) in seconds—eliminating the need for tedious manual weighing and database searches.  
**Product category:** AI Calorie & Food Tracker / Health & Fitness Mobile App (Süni İntellekt Dəstəkli Qida və Kalori İzləyicisi)  
**Product type:** B2C Mobile App (iOS & Android)  
**Business model:** Freemium B2C subscription tailored for Azerbaijan: Free core AI logging (up to 3 daily plate photo scans) with optional Pro tier at **4.99 AZN / month** or **39.99 AZN / year** (comparable to 1 cup of coffee in Baku). Currently in pre-launch / early access waitlist phase.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Traditional calorie counting in Azerbaijan is frustrating, tedious, and unsustainable. People either spend 15 minutes daily typing in foreign databases (FatSecret), pay 50–150 AZN/month for WhatsApp dietitians, or give up tracking entirely.  
**Why alternatives fall short in Azerbaijan:**
- **FatSecret**: 100% free, but requires manual kitchen scales and searches across confusing Russian/Turkish crowd-sourced lists.
- **YAZIO / MyFitnessPal**: Billed in foreign currency ($30–$80 USD = 50–140 AZN) with zero Azerbaijani food awareness.
- **Cal AI**: Aggressive $2.99/week USD paywalls; completely blind to Azerbaijani composite meals.
- **Instagram Dietitians**: Expensive (50–150 AZN/mo), delayed feedback (2–3 hours), and induces guilt on cheat meals.
**What it costs them:** Failed diets, lost progress, wasted time (15–30 min/day), and anxiety around food.  
**Emotional tension:** Guilt when skipping meal logs, frustration with complex UI, and diet fatigue.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- **Instant Photo Recognition:** Camera-first interface where a single snap identifies meals, portion size, and nutritional value.
- **Native Azerbaijani Localization & Regional Food Intelligence:** Built from the ground up for Azerbaijani users with local language support and awareness of regional cuisine.
- **Mindful, Aesthetic UI:** Soft ambient tones, elegant branding (Marck Script cursive touches), clean typography (Plus Jakarta Sans), and dark/light mode support.
- **Instant Macro Breakdown:** Protein, fat, carbs, and calorie balance displayed clearly without clutter.
**How we do it differently:** Replacing 10 manual steps (search -> weigh -> select -> input) with 1 click of the camera shutter.  
**Why that's better:** Removes 95% of tracking friction, dramatically boosting consistency and long-term success.  
**Why customers choose us:** Fast, effortless, beautiful, and localized.

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Can AI really accurately measure calories and portions from just a photo?" | AI assesses visual volume, ingredient density, and standard culinary portions. If a portion or ingredient is slightly off, you can adjust it with a single tap. |
| "Will it recognize Azerbaijani or homemade dishes (e.g., dolma, plov, kabab, bozbash)?" | Yes, bosq is tailored to recognize local cooking styles and traditional recipes, not just Western fast food. |
| "Is it free to use?" | You can join the early access waitlist for free today. Free core tracking will be available, alongside optional premium features for power users. |
| "What about privacy with my food photos?" | Photos are processed securely and privately for your personal nutritional log only. |

**Anti-persona:** Clinical medical patients needing exact hospital-grade nutrient metrics (e.g., severe renal or metabolic disorders requiring clinical laboratory precision) or people who refuse to use smartphones.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Tired of typing every ingredient, searching through thousands of wrong database entries, and giving up on fitness goals after two weeks.  
**Pull:** Snap a picture in 2 seconds and your meal is logged with exact macros and calorie count.  
**Habit:** Forgetting to log meals, or automatically opening MyFitnessPal despite hating the experience.  
**Anxiety:** "What if it miscalculates my calories?", "Will it work if I'm eating out with friends?"
