## 📌 Video Production Best Practices for Azerbaijan

1. **The 3-Second Rule**: Visual, Audio, and Text Hook MUST hit in the first 1.0 second.
2. **Subtitles (Altyazılar)**: 70%+ of Instagram Reels in Baku are watched on mute (work, metro, cafes). Use bold, centered 2-line subtitles in CapCut with black outline.
3. **Pacing**: Rapid cuts every 1.5–2.5 seconds. Never let a static screen linger.
4. **Local Cultural Authenticity**: Use recognizable Baku locations (Fountain Square, Port Baku, local cafes) or authentic home meals (*Təndir, Dolma, Plov*). Avoid generic stock video.

# Script 1: The Relatable Contrast ("FatSecret vs. bosq")

**Format**: Split-Screen / Quick Cut Contrast

**Trigger**: Cognitive dissonance & extreme friction vs. instant relief  
**Sound**: Trending energetic beat or funny sound effect transition

- **Text Overlay (Bold Yellow/White)**: *"2026-cı ildə hələ də yeməyi TƏRƏZİYƏ qoyursan? 🤦‍♂️"*
