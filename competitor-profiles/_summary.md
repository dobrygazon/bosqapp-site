## 4. The National Cuisine Barrier: Why Global AI Fails in Azerbaijan

Traditional Western nutrition algorithms are trained on segmented plates (e.g., grilled chicken breast + steamed broccoli + boiled brown rice). Azerbaijani food is complex, composite, rich in herbs, slow-cooked meats, and specific cooking oils:

| Azerbaijani Dish | How Western AI (Cal AI / YAZIO) Sees It | The Reality in Azerbaijani Kitchens | How bosq Solves It |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Yarpaq Dolması** | "Greek Dolmades" or "Vegetable Roll" (~150 kcal, mostly rice) | Packed with minced beef/mutton, onion, fresh herbs (coriander, dill, mint), fat, served with garlic qatıq (~450–600 kcal) | Identifies authentic Yarpaq Dolması, accounts for meat/fat ratio and standard portion count. |
| **Plov (Aş / Qovurma)** | "White Rice" or "Asian Fried Rice" (~250 kcal) | Cooked in clarified butter (*kərə yağı*), saffron, meat, chestnuts, dried apricots, plus crunchy *qazmaq* crust (700–900+ kcal) | Recognizes Azerbaijani plov varieties, detects *qara* toppings and estimates butter absorption. |
| **Qutab** (*Göyərti / Ət / Qarın*) | "Crepe", "Pancake", or "Tortilla" (~180 kcal) | Thin dough griddled on *sac*, brushed with butter, filled with mountain greens or seasoned meat; dusted with sumac (~280–380 kcal/pc) | Accurately identifies qutab category, filling type, and butter coating. |
| **Küftə-Bozbaş & Piti** | "Generic Beef Soup" or "Meatball Broth" | Piti is cooked in clay *dopu* with tail fat (*quyruq*), chickpeas, chestnuts, and saffron; Küftə contains a dried plum (*alça*) inside | Accounts for heavy broth calorie density, chickpeas, and hidden mutton fat. |
| **Pomidor-Yumurta & Təndir Çörəyi** | "Shakshuka" or "Scrambled Eggs with Salsa" | Stewed ripe tomatoes in generous butter with eggs, eaten with crusty clay-oven *təndir* bread | Evaluates egg-to-tomato ratio, cooking oil, and portion of tandir bread. |
| **Dovğa** | "Yogurt smoothie" or "Spinach soup" | Yogurt broth with rice, chickpeas, cilantro, dill, mint, and mountain herbs | Accurately logs light calorie profile and probiotic macro split. |

Foreign apps fail commercially in Azerbaijan because they price in US Dollars without purchasing-power parity. An app charging $2.99/week (~20 AZN/month) or $39.99/year (~68 AZN) encounters massive customer drop-off.

1. **Free Tier ("Həmişə Pulsuz")**:
   - Up to **3 AI photo meal scans per day** (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner).
   - Manual search fallback.
   - Daily calorie & macro target tracker.
   - *Why*: Eliminates the fear of being scammed; makes bosq the immediate default app for everyone currently struggling with FatSecret.
2. **bosq Premium (Pro Tier)**:
   - **Monthly**: **4.99 AZN / month** (less than the price of a single cup of coffee at Coffeeshop Company or Gloria Jean's in Baku).
   - **Annual**: **39.99 AZN / year** (~3.30 AZN/month).
   - *Features*: Unlimited AI plate scans, in-depth weekly nutrition trends, goal coaching (fat loss, clean bulk), restaurant meal advice, custom macro targets.
   - *Payment Integration*: Local cards (Birbank, Leobank, ABB, Pasha Bank) via local payment gateways (e.g. Payriff, GoldenPay, or localized Apple/Google Pay in AZN).
