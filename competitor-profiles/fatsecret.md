## Positioning & Messaging in Azerbaijan

**Local Perception**: *"The default, free food diary."*  
In Azerbaijan, whenever someone asks in a fitness forum, gym, or on Instagram *"Hansı kalori sayğacını işlədirsən?"* (Which calorie counter do you use?), the top answer is almost universally FatSecret. 

**Why it dominates locally**:
1. **Completely Free**: Unlike Western apps that lock tracking behind a paywall after 3 days, FatSecret allows unlimited daily manual logging for free.
2. **Post-Soviet & Turkish Database Access**: Because a large portion of Azerbaijani smartphone users are bilingual (Azerbaijani + Russian or Turkish), they search for foods in Russian (*"плов", "гречка", "куриная грудка"*) or Turkish (*"mercimek çorbası", "döner"*), which FatSecret has in abundance.

## Product & Features: Where It Fails in Azerbaijan

### Core capabilities
- Manual text search across international crowd-sourced databases.
- Barcode scanning (recognizes some imported Russian, Turkish, and multinational supermarket goods found in Bravo, Rahat, or Bazarstore).
- Daily calorie and macro tallying (Proteins, Fats, Carbs).

### The Azerbaijani User's Frustrations & Pain Points
1. **Zero AI Photo Recognition**: Users must weigh every item on a kitchen scale and manually type each ingredient. In Azerbaijani culture, where family meals and social gatherings are central, nobody can weigh their food at dinner.
2. **The "Azerbaijani Dish" Nightmare**:
   - Searching *"Yarpaq dolması"* returns 10 conflicting community entries created by random users: one says 120 kcal, another says 450 kcal, another is in Russian (*"долма из виноградных листьев"*).
   - Users get overwhelmed: *"Hansını seçim?"* (Which one do I choose?).
   - Dishes like *Piti, Küftə-bozbaş, Səbzi qovurma*, or *Düşbərə* have wild calorie discrepancies because crowd-sourced recipes don't match home cooking.
3. **Outdated, Depressing Interface**:
   - The app feels like a 2012 spreadsheet with intrusive banner ads.
   - It turns eating into a stressful bookkeeping task. 85%+ of Azerbaijani users drop out within 10 to 14 days due to sheer logging fatigue.
4. **No Native Azerbaijani Interface**:
   - Forces users to navigate in Russian, Turkish, or English, excluding users who strictly prefer Azerbaijani.

## Strengths & Weaknesses (Azerbaijan Market)

### Strengths
- **Dominant Word-of-Mouth Habit**: Established default recommendation among Baku personal trainers.
- **Zero Cost Barrier**: Free tier means users don't fear credit card deductions.
- **Large Post-Soviet Grocery Database**: Recognizes common CIS dairy (tvorog, kefir) and Turkish imports.

### Weaknesses
- **Extreme Time Drain**: Requires 15–25 minutes of manual typing every day.
- **No Photo Capability**: Impossible to use discreetly at restaurants, weddings, or family dinners.
- **High Churn Rate**: Extreme drop-off rate as users get burned out from manual entry.
- **Lack of Azerbaijani Cultural Pride**: Old, impersonal foreign software.
