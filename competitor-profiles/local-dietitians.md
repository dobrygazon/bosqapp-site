## Why This Service Is So Popular in Azerbaijan

1. **Human Accountability**: Azerbaijani users often say *"Məni kimsə yoxlamasa, pəhriz saxlaya bilmirəm"* (If someone doesn't check on me, I can't stay on a diet).
2. **Culturally Tailored Menus**: Local dietitians understand Azerbaijani foods: they specify exact portions of *təndir çörəyi*, *qatıq*, *motal pendiri*, or *toyuq ləvəngisi*.
3. **No Tech Friction**: The user doesn't have to navigate a complex app database; they simply open WhatsApp, snap a photo, and hit send.

---

## Where Human Dietitians Fall Short (Why Users Quit)

1. **Very High Monthly Cost**: At 50–150 AZN/month, it is unaffordable for university students, young professionals, and budget-conscious families.
2. **Delayed Feedback**: If you are at a restaurant or lunch break, the dietitian might take 2–3 hours to reply to your WhatsApp photo. By then, you have already finished eating.
3. **Guilt & Shame**: Users feel embarrassed to send photos of "cheat meals" or holiday feasts (Novruz sweets, weddings, birthday dinners), leading them to hide meals or abandon the program.
4. **Subjective Eyeballing**: Most local dietitians just guess based on sight without computing real macronutrient grams (protein/fat/carb targets).
