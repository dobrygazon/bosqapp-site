# SnapCalorie — User Reviews & Feedback Analysis
**Source**: Apple App Store, Product Hunt, Y Combinator Community
**Date**: 2026-09-09
**Average Rating**: ~4.7 / 5.0 (App Store)
**Ratings Count**: ~1,500+ ratings

## Positive Feedback Themes
1. **Free Tier Generosity**: Unlike Cal AI, users appreciate that they can snap up to 3 AI photos per day without being forced into an instant weekly subscription.
2. **LiDAR & Depth Technology**: Tech enthusiasts praise the volume estimation accuracy on iPhone Pro models.
3. **Voice Logging**: Praised by cooking enthusiasts and busy parents who cannot type while preparing meals.
4. **Research Heritage**: Strong trust due to Google Lens pedigree and published CVPR research papers.

## Negative Feedback & Complaints
1. **Learning Curve on Angles**: Achieving high accuracy requires taking photos from specific 45-degree angles or capturing plate borders.
2. **Expensive Pro Subscription**: Upgrading to unlimited scans costs $79.99 - $149/year, which many users find prohibitively expensive compared to standard fitness apps.
3. **Regional Cuisine Recognition**: USDA database and training sets struggle with non-Western / regional ethnic dishes.
4. **App Interface Polish**: The UI feels more utilitarian and engineering-focused rather than welcoming or aesthetically soothing.
