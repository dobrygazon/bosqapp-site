## Technology & Positioning Pillars
- **Computer Vision & LiDAR 3D Depth**: Measures volume of food using iPhone Pro LiDAR sensors + algorithms from Nutrition5k CVPR 2021 paper.
- **Voice Notes**: Hands-free voice commands to log meals or describe food.
- **Free Meal Planner**: Built-in web tool generating custom diet plans (Anything, Keto, Mediterranean, Paleo, Vegan, Vegetarian).
- **Free Forever Promise**: Core AI photo tracking is free for up to 3 scans/day.
- **B2B API Offering**: Developer API available at $499/month for 10,000 requests.
- **Founder Credibility**: Co-founded by Wade Norris (former Google Lens co-founder) and Scott Baron.
