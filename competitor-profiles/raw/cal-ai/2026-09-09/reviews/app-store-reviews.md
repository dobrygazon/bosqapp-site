# Cal AI — User Reviews & Sentiment Analysis
**Source**: Apple App Store, Google Play Store, Reddit (r/CICO, r/loseit, r/Fitness)
**Date**: 2026-09-09
**Average Rating**: ~4.8 / 5.0 (App Store), ~4.6 / 5.0 (Google Play)
**Ratings Count**: 150,000+ ratings across stores

## Positive Feedback Themes
1. **Speed & Convenience**: Users love being able to snap pictures of food at work or when dining out without searching databases manually.
2. **Visual Simplicity**: Modern, clean, and gamified interface that makes tracking feel less like bookkeeping.
3. **Macro Estimation**: Provides reasonable estimates for protein/carb/fat splits for simple, well-lit single-plate meals.

## Negative Feedback & Complaints
1. **Paywall Pressure & Recurring Charges**: Users complain about being aggressively pushed into subscriptions during onboarding ($2.99/week or $29.99/year) without clear notice that the AI camera won't function without paying.
2. **Mixed & Cultural Foods Accuracy**: Significant inaccuracies when meals have hidden ingredients (oils, sauces, mixed stews, regional dishes, or rice dishes like plov/biryani).
3. **Food Database Depth**: Traditional text database lookup has fewer entries and fewer verified regional items compared to legacy apps like MyFitnessPal.
4. **App Performance**: Some users report high battery consumption and device warming during camera usage.
