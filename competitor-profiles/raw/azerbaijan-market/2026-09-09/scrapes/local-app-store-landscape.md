# Azerbaijan Mobile App Landscape — Calorie & Nutrition Trackers
**Market**: Azerbaijan (Baku & Regions)  
**Distribution**: Exclusive to Azerbaijan  
**Date**: 2026-09-09  
**Platforms**: Apple App Store (AZ Storefront), Google Play Store (AZ)

## Key Apps Observed in Azerbaijan
1. **FatSecret (Calorie Counter by fatsecret)**
   - Status: The most widely used manual calorie tracker in Azerbaijan.
   - Drivers: 100% free basic functionality, strong presence of Russian and Turkish database entries which local users use as substitutes for Azerbaijani dishes.
   - Interface: Outdated, table-heavy, tedious manual search.

2. **YAZIO (AI Calorie Counter & Fasting)**
   - Status: Popular among Baku youth, millennials, and female fitness audiences.
   - Drivers: Modern visual UI, intermittent fasting trackers, clean design.
   - Pain points: Billed in foreign currency ($USD/EUR), expensive premium tiers, AI camera is Western-focused and struggles with local cuisine.

3. **Cal AI (by Viral Development LLC / MyFitnessPal)**
   - Status: Highly visible via TikTok & Instagram Reels ads running in Azerbaijan.
   - Drivers: High curiosity for "just snap a photo" AI tracking.
   - Pain points: English only, zero Azerbaijani language support, misidentifies national dishes (plov, dolma, bozbash, qutab), aggressive weekly subscription traps ($2.99/week ~ 5 AZN/week = 20 AZN/month) causing immediate uninstalls.

4. **Emerging Regional Experiments (KKAL AI, Caloris, Kaloria, Nutix)**
   - Status: Early-stage attempts to capture regional users.
   - Observations: Low brand awareness, varying quality of AI models, often simple wrappers around generic vision APIs without deep cultural fine-tuning.

5. **Local Human Alternatives (Instagram & WhatsApp Dietitians / "Dietoloqlar")**
   - High-spending demographic pays 40–150 AZN/month for private WhatsApp chat groups where they send photos of their plates to a nutritionist who manually critiques or logs meals.
