# Local Azerbaijani Consumer Sentiment & Food Habits Analysis
**Date**: 2026-09-09  
**Target Market**: Azerbaijan (Baku, Sumqayit, Ganja, and regional urban hubs)

## Food Culture & AI Recognition Challenges in Azerbaijan
Azerbaijani cuisine presents unique challenges that break generic Western vision models:
1. **Plov (Aş / Shah Plov / Səbzi Qovurma Plov)**:
   - Western models classify as: "White rice" or "Asian fried rice".
   - Reality: Cooked with clarified butter (kərə yağı), saffron, meat, chestnuts, dried apricots/plums, and tahdig/qazmaq crust. Calorie variance is massive (250 kcal vs 800+ kcal per plate).
2. **Dolma (Yarpaq Dolması / Üç Bacı Dolması)**:
   - Western models classify as: "Greek dolmades" (often olive-oil/rice based) or "stuffed peppers".
   - Reality: Azerbaijani yarpaq dolması contains fatty minced mutton/beef, cilantro, dill, mint, onion, with high protein and fat density; eaten with qatıq (garlic yogurt).
3. **Qutab (Göyərti / Ət / Balqabaq / Qarın)**:
   - Western models classify as: "Pancake", "Crepe", or "Tortilla".
   - Reality: Thin unleavened dough griddled on a sac, brushed with butter, filled with herbs, meat, or pumpkin; dusted with sumac.
4. **Şorbalar (Piti / Küftə-Bozbaş / Düşbərə / Dovğa)**:
   - Piti is cooked in clay pots (dopu) with mutton, chickpeas, chestnuts, saffron, and a chunk of tail fat (quyruq). Generic AI fails completely to estimate the quyruq and broth calorie density.
   - Dovğa is cold/hot yogurt-based with rice, chickpeas, and fresh greens. AI labels it as "smoothie" or "spinach dip".
5. **Səhər yeməyi (Pomidor-Yumurta & Təndir çörəyi)**:
   - AI confuses pomidor-yumurta with Mexican salsa or shakshuka; misses butter quantity used in Azerbaijani homes.

## Local Pricing Psychology (AZN)
- **Willingness to Pay**: Consumers in Azerbaijan are accustomed to digital subscription benchmarks:
  - Spotify Premium: ~4.99 USD (~8.50 AZN/month)
  - YouTube Premium: ~6–9 AZN/month
  - Local gym memberships: 40–100 AZN/month
- **Rejection of USD Paywalls**: An app charging $2.99/week (~20 AZN/month) or $39.99/year (~68 AZN upfront) without local pricing triggers immediate cancellation and negative store reviews ("Pulumu kəsdi", "Çox bahadır", "Almaniya/ABŞ qiymətləridir").
- **Optimal Local Price Point**: Free core tier (e.g. 3 AI scans/day) + Pro tier at **4.99 AZN / month** or **39.99 AZN / year** (comparable to 1-2 cups of coffee in Baku).
