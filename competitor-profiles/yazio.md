## Where YAZIO Falls Short in the Azerbaijani Market

1. **Foreign Currency & High Subscription Pricing**:
   - YAZIO frequently pushes annual subscriptions costing $29.99 – $49.99 USD (~50 – 85 AZN upfront).
   - In Azerbaijan, where minimum monthly wage is ~345 AZN and average youth discretionary budgets are tight, spending 50–80+ AZN on a single app purchase creates massive sticker shock.
   - Users frequently leave 1-star reviews on the App Store: *"Pulunu kəsdi, ləğv edə bilmirəm"* (Deducted money, can't cancel).
2. **Useless AI Camera for Azerbaijani Dishes**:
   - YAZIO includes a camera feature ("Smart Food Recognition"), but it is trained predominantly on Central European, German, and Western ingredients (muesli, bratwurst, pre-packaged sandwiches, European salads).
   - When tested on Azerbaijani meals:
     - It confuses *Kabab* with generic grilled sausage or beef patties.
     - It cannot classify *Pomidor-yumurta*, *Dovğa*, or *Qutab*.
     - It fails completely on home-cooked composite stews like *Səbzi qovurma* or *Piti*.
   - Users are forced to revert to manual typing.
3. **No Native Azerbaijani Language**:
   - Despite offering Turkish and Russian, it completely ignores the Azerbaijani language and local cultural context.
